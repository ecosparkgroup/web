About ECOSPARK

From coconut shell to a broader carbon platform.

COCONUT SHELL → CARBON → MATERIALS → APPLICATIONS

We believe renewable biomass should be more than a feedstock. It can be the starting point for a new generation of measurable, application-driven carbon materials.

ECOSPARK is building its carbon-materials platform around coconut shell, one of the most valuable renewable biomass feedstocks for carbon production. Our model connects sourcing, carbonization, processing and quality control to create products for different levels of the carbon value chain — from established commercial markets to future advanced materials.

For international B2B customers, we focus on the information that matters before a commercial decision: product specification, testing evidence, packaging, logistics, traceability, application fit, supply capability and a clear route to enquiry.

Core Strategic Focus

Built around consistency, purity and operational reliability.

ECOSPARK is positioning itself as a technology-driven biomass carbon and biochar manufacturer. Our advantage is not only the feedstock itself, but the discipline with which we control the journey from raw material to finished product.

01 · FEEDSTOCKFarm-to-factory sourcing and raw material screening to reduce dirt, sand, moisture and other contamination.
02 · AUTOMATIONPLC-controlled continuous pyrolysis with monitored temperature and residence time for more consistent carbonization.
03 · TQMIQC → PQC → FQC quality control, supported by batch-level documentation and laboratory verification.
04 · DISCIPLINE5S workplace controls, resource-efficient processing and syngas recycling where applicable.
05 · SERVICEEnd-to-end commercial, packaging and export logistics support with technical sales assistance.
Quality Assurance Framework

Quality is controlled at every stage.

Our TQM framework is designed to prevent quality problems rather than discover them only at the end of production.

01

IQC

Incoming raw feedstock inspection and screening before production.

02

PQC

In-process monitoring of carbonization parameters and production consistency.

03

FQC

Final product verification before shipment against agreed specifications.

04

COA

Batch documentation supported by relevant laboratory test results.

Typical quality parameters may include Fixed Carbon, Ash Content, Volatile Matter and Moisture. Final specifications and testing methods will be confirmed against approved product master data.
